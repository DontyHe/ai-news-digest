## Research Themes (2025-2026)

Kaiming He's recent work clusters around several themes:

### 1. Generative Models / Mean Flows
- Multiple papers on "Mean Flows" — a new approach to generative modeling
- Focus on one-step generation (avoiding iterative sampling overhead)
- Bidirectional normalizing flows
- Fast forward generative models

### 2. Visual Pre-training / Self-Supervised Learning
- MAE (Masked Autoencoders) continues to be influential
- Work on "Pixio" — enhanced MAE with data curation for dense prediction
- "In Pursuit of Pixel Supervision for Visual Pre-training" at CVPR 2026

### 3. Foundation Models for Vision
- Image generators as vision learners
- VLMs (Vision-Language Models) with heterogeneous bottleneck adapters (HeBA)
- Scaling physics simulation

### 4. Efficient Architectures
- Heterogeneous adapters for CLIP adaptation
- Parameter-efficient fine-tuning
- Fast inference methods
