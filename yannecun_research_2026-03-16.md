### 1. Temporal Straightening for Latent Planning

### 2. The Spike, the Sparse and the Sink: Anatomy of Massive Activations and Attention Sinks
- **arXiv**: 2603.05498
- **Authors**: Shangwen Sun, Alfredo Canziani, Yann LeCun, Jiachen Zhu
- **Category**: cs.AI, cs.CL
- **Abstract**: 研究Transformer语言模型中的两种现象：massive activations和attention sinks。发现两者在modern Transformer design中经常共同出现是architectural artifact，但服务不同功能。Massive activations operate globally，attention sinks operate locally。Pre-norm configuration是关键选择。
- **Submitted**: 5 March 2026

### 4. Beyond Language Modeling: An Exploration of Multimodal Pretraining
- **arXiv**: 2603.03276
- **Authors**: Shengbang Tong, David Fan, John Nguyen, Ellis Brown, Gaoyue Zhou, Shengyi Qian, Boyang Zheng, Théophane Vallaeys, Junlin Han, Rob Fergus, Naila Murray, Marjan Ghazvininejad, Mike Lewis, Nicolas Ballas, Amir Bar, Michael Rabbat, Jakob Verbeek, Luke Zettlemoyer, Koustuv Sinha, Yann LeCun, Saining Xie
- **Category**: cs.CV
- **Abstract**: 使用Transfusion框架（language用next-token prediction，vision用diffusion）进行from-scratch multimodal pretraining。四个关键发现：RAE提供最佳统一视觉表示；视觉和语言数据互补；统一多模态pretraining自然导向world modeling；MoE实现高效多模态scaling。发现scaling asymmetry：vision比language更data-hungry。
- **Submitted**: 3 March 2026
