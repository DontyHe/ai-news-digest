## Highlights

- **JEPA family expanding rapidly**: HP-JEPA (graph), Music-JEPA (audio), AdaJEPA (adaptive world model), SkyJEPA (robotics/quadrotors), S-JEPA (speech). The JEPA framework is being applied across modalities.
- **Embodied AI relevant papers**:
  - **Patch Policy** (Jul 20): Efficient embodied control using dense visual representations. Directly relevant to VLA research.
  - **SkyJEPA** (Jun 22): Long-horizon world models for zero-shot sim-to-real quadrotor control.
- **HP-JEPA** (Aug 1): Extends JEPA to graphs with hierarchical partitioning for multi-resolution learning.
- **Temporal Differences for Vision** (Jun 14): Claims strong visual representations can be learned without strong assumptions, just from temporal differences. Challenges the need for explicit augmentation strategies.
