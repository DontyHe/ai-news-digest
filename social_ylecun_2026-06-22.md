# Yann LeCun (@ylecun) - Social Media Digest
**Date**: 2026-06-22

## Recent Activity

### Yann LeCun calls xAI a "failure" (June 18-20, 2026)
In a CNBC interview, LeCun made several high-profile statements:

1. **On xAI**: Called it "kind of a failure, frankly" because the founding team has left or was fired. Said Musk finds it "very, very difficult" to hire top AI talent due to how he treated previous team members.

2. **On xAI economics**: xAI has "huge infrastructure" rented to others because "that's the only way he can recoup the cost." Anthropic and Google both rent compute at Colossus data centres in Memphis, with Google paying SpaceX ~$920mn/month.

3. **On AI industry bubble**: Warned that AI services are getting more expensive to run than labs can charge for. "The prices are going up... but the cost of running them is going down, but not nearly fast enough. All of those companies are losing money, and basically, the use for most people is funded by the investors."

4. **Predicted outcomes**: Labs "are going to have to increase prices, cut costs, or there's going to be a big bubble explosion."

5. **AMI Labs context**: In March 2026, his startup AMI Labs raised ~$1bn on the thesis that LLMs are a dead end. He argues the future belongs to "world models" and that "we're not going to have generalized reliable agentic systems" without them.

6. **Musk feud**: Musk has called LeCun "out of touch with AI for a long time." The two have feuded for years.

### Background
- Founder of AMI Labs
- Former Chief AI Scientist at Meta (FAIR)
- Professor at NYU
- Turing Award recipient (2018)
- Advocating for Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA) and world models as alternative to LLMs
