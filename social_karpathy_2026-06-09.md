### 2. Sequoia Ascent 2026 Talk (April 30, 2026)
Key themes from his fireside chat with Stephanie Zhan:

**Software 3.0 Paradigm**
- Software 1.0 = writing code
- Software 2.0 = programming by creating datasets/training neural networks
- Software 3.0 = programming through prompting (context window as lever, LLM as interpreter)

**December 2025 Inflection Point**
- First noticed that with latest models, chunks of work "just came out fine"
- "I couldn't remember the last time I corrected it"
- Coherent agentic workflows started to actually work

**Jagged Intelligence**
- LLMs are "jagged entities" that peak in verifiable domains (math, code) and stagnate elsewhere
- Traditional computers automate what you can specify in code; LLMs automate what you can verify
- Verifiability makes something tractable in current paradigm

**Vibe Coding vs Agentic Engineering**
- Vibe coding raises the floor for everyone
- Agentic engineering is about extrapolating the ceiling
- "10x engineer" is old framing; the ceiling is much higher now
- "You can outsource your thinking, but you can't outsource your understanding"

### 5. Recent Podcast: "Decade of Agents, Limits of RL" (March 2026)
**Key Quotes**:
- "Current RL for LLMs is crude: you generate hundreds of parallel attempts, check which got the right answer, and upweight every single token in those trajectories — even the wrong turns"
- Calls it "sucking supervision through a straw"
- Process-based supervision is the fix but tricky to implement (LLM judges have adversarial vulnerabilities)
- Synthetic data generation silently collapses distribution
- **Contrarian prediction**: A genuinely intelligent cognitive core could fit in ~1 billion parameters (current models ~1 trillion, most capacity is memorizing facts)
- Does not expect AI to cause visible discontinuity in economic growth (transformative techs are invisible in GDP curves)
