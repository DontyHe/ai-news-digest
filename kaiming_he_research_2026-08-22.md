## Highlights

- **MobileWAM** (Aug 5): Connects World Action Models to mobile manipulation via chain-of-foresight planning. Relevant to embodied AI / VLA research.
- **Video/Image Generators as Vision Learners** (Jul/Apr): Two papers arguing generation models can serve as general-purpose vision backbones, continuing the trend of generative → discriminative transfer.
- **Generative Modeling direction**: Multiple papers on flow-based and mean-flow generative models (ELF, Drifting, Pixel Mean Flows, Bidirectional NF), suggesting a shift away from diffusion toward flow-based approaches.
- **ARC Is a Vision Problem!**: Claims the ARC benchmark is fundamentally a vision task rather than pure reasoning.
