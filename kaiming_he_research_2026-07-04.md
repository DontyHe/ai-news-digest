## Recent Papers (Latest Updates)

### 1. ELF: Embedded Language Flows (Updated v2, Jun 26)
- **arXiv**: 2605.10938v2
- **Published**: 2026-05-11, updated 2026-06-26
- **Authors**: Keya Hu, Linlu Qiu, Yiyang Lu, Hanhong Zhao, Tianhong Li, Yoon Kim, Jacob Andreas, **Kaiming He**
- **Categories**: cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG
- **Summary**: Continuous-time Flow Matching diffusion model operating in continuous embedding space until final step, then mapping to discrete tokens via shared-weight network. Can adapt image-domain techniques (e.g., CFG). v2 adds distillation results in Appendix B.
- **Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10938v2
- **Page**: https://linlu-qiu.github.io/assets/html/elf_pd.html

### 2. Image Generators are Generalist Vision Learners (Vision Banana)
- **arXiv**: 2604.20329v3
- **Published**: 2026-04-22, updated 2026-06-03
- **Authors**: Valentin Gabeur, Shangbang Long, Songyou Peng, ..., **Kaiming He**, Thomas Funkhouser, Jean-Baptiste Alayrac, Radu Soricut
- **Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI
- **Summary**: Demonstrates image generation training enables powerful general visual representations. Vision Banana instruction-tunes Nano Banana Pro to reframe perception as image generation. SOTA on 2D/3D understanding tasks, beating SAM3 and Depth Anything.
- **Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20329v3
- **Page**: http://vision-banana.github.io

### 3. GeoPT: Scaling Physics Simulation via Lifted Geometric Pre-Training
- **arXiv**: 2602.20399v2
- **Published**: 2026-02-23, updated 2026-05-20
- **Authors**: Haixu Wu, Minghao Guo, Zongyi Li, ..., **Kaiming He**, Wojciech Matusik
- **Categories**: cs.LG
- **Summary**: Lifted geometric pre-training with synthetic dynamics for neural simulation. Pre-trained on 1M+ samples. Reduces labeled data needs 20-60%, 2x convergence acceleration on industrial fluid/solid mechanics benchmarks.
- **Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20399v2
- **Code**: https://github.com/Physics-Scaling/GeoPT
